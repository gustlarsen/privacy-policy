Summary: "How Predictable You Are" is an offline casual strategy puzzle game. The application does not collect, track, transmit, share, or sell any personal information or sensitive user data. All game calculations and progress are executed and stored strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data.

When you download and play "How Predictable You Are", we do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts). The app operates completely standalone without requesting user identification.

2. Device Permissions Used

The application only requests the minimum system permissions necessary to provide its core gaming experience:

Permission Purpose Type / Sensitivity
android.permission.VIBRATE Used exclusively to deliver tactile haptic feedback when pressing game buttons and during match outcome animations. Users can disable vibration at any time within the game settings. Normal / Non-sensitive

The application does NOT request or use:

  • Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested in the application manifest)
  • Location permissions (precise or approximate GPS)
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Device contacts, accounts, or call logs
  • External file system access or media storage access
  • Advertising identifiers (GAID / AAID)

3. Local Data Storage & Gameplay State

To enable progression across sessions, the application stores gameplay parameters locally on your device sandbox using standard platform preferences (SharedPreferences):

  • Game Statistics: Win/loss counts, total rounds played, and earned in-game diamonds.
  • Markov Engine State: Discrete transition frequency counts representing moves played in Rock-Paper-Scissors (used solely for the local algorithm to predict subsequent moves).
  • Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effects, background music), vibration settings, and language selection.
  • In-Game Unlocks: Toggles for optional UI features (e.g. Hint bar, visual matrix inspector) unlocked via in-game diamonds.

All stored state is sandboxed locally on your hardware. It is never synchronized to cloud servers or transmitted outside your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics platforms, including but not limited to:

  • No Google Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics
  • No AdMob or third-party ad networks
  • No Facebook/Meta SDKs
  • No user behavior telemetry or tracking cookies

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your data on external servers, we hold no user data to retain or delete. You retain complete control over your local data:

  • In-App Reset: You can clear your game scores and Markov transition history directly within the game.
  • Device Settings: You can wipe all stored preferences at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > How Predictable You Are > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling: Removing the app permanently deletes all associated local data from your device.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Protecting children's privacy is extremely important to us. "How Predictable You Are" does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Security

We care about safeguarding your device and experience. Because the application runs entirely offline and retains all game variables within the secure Android application sandbox, your gameplay data remains isolated from unauthorized external access.
